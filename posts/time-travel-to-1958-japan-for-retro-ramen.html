Time Travel to 1958 Japan for Retro Ramen! 🍜✨

📍 Kumamoto Ramen Komurasaki Shin Yokohama

Watch on YouTube →

Ever wondered what it feels like to step straight into a time machine? On our latest adventure, we took a trip to the **Shin-Yokohama Ramen Museum** in Yokohama, Japan—a place that transports you back to 1958, the year instant ramen was invented.

As soon as you head downstairs, you’re immersed in a breathtaking, two-story replica of post-war Showa-era Tokyo. The glowing neon shop signs, narrow back alleys, vintage phone booths, and nostalgic retro advertisements make you feel like you’ve stepped onto an active historic movie set.

---

**The Ultimate Retro Foodie Experience at Komurasaki**

After exploring the alleys, it was time for the main event: **Ramen!**

The museum hosts famous ramen shops from across Japan, and we stopped at **Komurasaki**, a legendary spot originally from Kumamoto:

* **Ordering retro-style:** We grabbed our meal tickets using ticket machines outside the shop before heading over to the counter.
* **Watching the masters at work:** We got a front-row seat watching the chefs prepare fresh, steaming bowls right in the open kitchen.
* **The verdict:** Komurasaki’s signature Kumamoto-style tonkotsu broth with roasted garlic oil, tender chashu pork, wood-ear mushrooms, and perfectly cooked noodles made for pure comfort food heaven!

---

If you are traveling through Japan and love a mix of history, unique ambiance, and mouth-watering food, the Shin-Yokohama Ramen Museum is an absolute must-add to your bucket list. It’s not just dinner—it’s a full-on journey back in time!
