# This Japanese Vending Machine Makes REAL Coffee ☕🇯🇵

If you’ve traveled around Japan, you already know that vending machines (*jidouhanbeiki*) are practically on every corner. From ice-cold green teas to hot canned coffees, they’re an essential part of daily life. But during our latest spot for **Emmerican Adventure**, we ran into a specific type of vending machine that takes Japan’s street-side convenience to an entirely new level: **a machine that grinds and brews REAL fresh coffee right in front of your eyes.**

---

---

## Not Your Average Canned Coffee Machine

While canned coffee from Boss or Georgia is iconic in its own right, these specialty cup-dispensing machines (often branded under Georgia or Apex) operate like an automated micro-cafe.

Instead of popping out a pre-made aluminum can, the machine goes through an entire freshly brewed drip process:

1. **Customization Controls:** Before brewing, you can adjust your preferences right on the control panel—selecting your desired sweetness level, amount of cream/milk, and coffee strength.
2. **Watch the Drip:** The machine features a clear glass viewing window illuminated by LED lights. As soon as you press start, you can literally watch the coffee beans get ground, dropped into the filter, and freshly dripped.
3. **Automated Door:** Once the pour is complete, the protective glass door opens automatically to reveal a steaming cup of freshly brewed coffee, complete with real crema or microfoam.

## Why Japan's Vending Machine Culture Hits Different

What makes these machines so memorable for travelers in Japan isn't just the novelty—it's the surprising quality. For around 160 to 200 yen (roughly $1.00 to $1.40 USD), you get a cup of coffee that easily rivals standard coffee shop drip coffee, available 24/7 inside train stations, rest stops, and office buildings.

Whether you're grabbing a quick morning pick-me-up before hopping on the Shinkansen or stopping for an afternoon treat while exploring, keeping an eye out for these fresh-drip coffee machines is a must-try experience on your Japan itinerary!
