What It’s Really Like Shopping at Costco in Yokohama, Japan 🇯🇵🛒

📍 Costco Wholesale Kanazawa Seaside Warehouse

Watch on YouTube →

A Taste of Home in Japan: Exploring Costco YokohamaMoving overseas brings endless exciting opportunities to sample local culture, but every now and then, a little familiar comfort goes a long way. For expats living in Japan, one of the best bridges between American warehouse scale and Japanese variety is a weekend run to Costco Wholesale.

On a recent trip up to the Yokohama location, we took a stroll through the aisles to see how the big-box shopping experience translates across the Pacific. Whether you are living locally, planning a PCS move, or just curious about international warehouse club quirks, stepping inside a Japanese Costco is an adventure all its own.

Getting In and Moving Around

The familiar red membership counter greets you right at the door. One of the best perks for military families and expats is that Costco honors memberships globally. If you already have an active card from the States, it scans right at the entrance and register without missing a beat.

Navigating multi-story retail stores in Japan is always a treat, and Costco is no exception. Moving between floors means loading your cart onto specialized flat travelator ramps. The cart wheels lock securely into place as the incline moves, letting you ride hands-free between parking decks and the main sales floor without worrying about runaway groceries.

Familiar Staples Meets Local Flavors

Walking the main floor feels like a blend of standard warehouse bulk and local supermarket treasures. You can still grab giant paper towel bundles, household cleaning supplies, and familiar pantry goods, but the real fun begins when exploring the regional products lining the shelves.

The fresh deli and seafood sections feature massive trays of nigiri sushi, sashimi, roll platters, and seasonal Japanese desserts sitting right alongside classic Kirkland bakery items. The pantry aisles stay stocked with bulk green teas, regional miso pastes, dried seaweed varieties, and local snack packs alongside massive bags of American chips and chocolates. It makes balancing weeknight home cooking and traditional Japanese meal prep remarkably easy.

The Food Court Local Menu

No Costco run is complete without hitting the food court on the way out. While you can still score the classic quarter-pound hot dog and soda combo for an unbeatable price, the local menu additions are where things get really interesting.

Instead of just standard pizza options, the Yokohama food court serves up localized specialties like the Jumbo Bulgogi Bake, fresh Salmon Poke Rolls, and crispy Chicken Sticks. Grabbing a quick bite after packing the trunk offers a fun fusion of back-home nostalgia and local flavor.

If you are navigating life in Japan and looking for a quick comfort refill, a trip to Costco in Yokohama delivers the perfect mix of bulk essentials and unique local finds.
