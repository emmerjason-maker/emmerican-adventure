nside a Massive Japanese Sporting Goods Store: Exploring Super Sports Xebio in Yokosuka

📍 Super Sports Xebio L-Breath

Since moving to Yokosuka, exploring Japan’s huge local retail stores has quickly become one of the best parts of getting settled into our new routine. For a sports-loving family, stepping inside Super Sports Xebio feels less like a typical retail errand and more like walking into an athletic wonderland.

Endless Aisles of Gear & Sports Fashion

Right through the main entrance—complete with rustic wood beams and life-sized wildlife displays—you are met with row after row of gear spanning virtually every sport imaginable. One of the first stops is an enormous wall of fitted and snapback hats stocked with everything from Major League Baseball franchises to local Japanese favorites, highlighting just how seriously sports fashion is taken across Japan.

A Next-Level Baseball Department

With baseball being a huge part of our family's life, the store’s massive baseball section is easily the highlight of the trip. Compared to standard big-box sporting goods stores back in the States, the variety and craftsmanship on display here are on another level:

  • Wall-to-Wall Fielding Gloves: Entire floor-to-ceiling shelving units are packed with hundreds of gloves featuring intricate webbings, premium Japanese leather, and bold, vibrant colorways you rarely see lined up in one place.

  • Cleats, Bats & Training Gear: Alongside rows of Mizuno, ASICS, and ZETT footwear, the aisles are loaded with bats, batting gloves, protective gear, and specialized training aids.

Everything from Running Shoes to a Full Golf Pro Shop

Beyond the baseball diamond, Super Sports Xebio covers every corner of active life:

  • Activewear & Running Footwear: Massive apparel sections offer workout clothes and athletic gear for both adults and kids, paired with an entire wall dedicated to running shoes and court sneakers.

  • Victoria Golf Pro Shop: Connected right into the store footprint is a dedicated, high-end golf department complete with indoor putting greens, racks of clubs, golf bags, and pro-shop accessories.

Whether you are shopping for serious travel-ball gear or just browsing to see how Japanese retail does sports, Super Sports Xebio is an easy afternoon stop worth checking out.
