Ultimate Comfort Food: A Night Out at CoCo Ichibanya

📍 CoCo Ichibanya

Watch on YouTube →

If you have ever visited Japan or explored Japanese comfort food, chances are you’ve come across **CoCo Ichibanya** (*CoCoICHI*). Widely celebrated as the go-to spot for customized Japanese curry, it offers an experience that is equal parts delicious, satisfying, and uniquely tailored to your taste.

In our latest video, [Ultimate Japanese Katsu Curry Experience at CoCo Ichibanya! 🍛✨](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=alkyXP1DVRo), we took a trip to CoCo Ichibanya to show you what makes dining here such a standout experience!

---

### Step 1: The Touchscreen Ordering Experience [[00:03](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D3)]

One of the best parts about dining at modern CoCo Ichibanya locations is the ease of ordering. The interactive touchscreens allow you to switch the language to English [[00:06](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D6)] and fully customize your meal step-by-step:

* **Choose Your Curry Base & Rice Amount:** Whether you want standard rice portioning or a light meal, you control the base.
* **Select Your Spice Level:** From completely mild to fire-breathing level 10 spice, you can set the exact heat level to suit your palate.
* **Pick Your Toppings:** Options range from classic crispy tonkatsu (breaded pork cutlet) [[00:06](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D6)] and chicken cutlet to hamburgers, vegetables, and stewed meats.

---

### Step 2: The Main Event – Crispy Katsu Curry [[00:07](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D7)]

When the main dish arrives at the table, it steams with rich, aromatic curry sauce poured over fluffy Japanese rice alongside a perfectly golden, extra-crispy katsu cutlet [[00:25](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D25)].

The balance of textures is what makes this meal unforgettable:

* A golden-brown panko breadcrumb crust that holds its crunch [[00:30](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D30)].
* Tender, juicy meat on the inside.
* Savory, thick curry gravy packed with potatoes, carrots, and green beans [[00:54](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D54)].

---

### Step 3: Elevate Your Plate with Table Condiments [[00:33](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D33)]

No CoCo Ichibanya visit is complete without making full use of the table condiments!

1. **Fukujinzuke (Pickled Vegetables):** [[00:33](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D33)] These sweet and crunchy pickled radishes act as the perfect palate cleanser between bites of rich curry.
2. **Special Aromatic Sauce (芳醇ソース):** [[00:37](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D37)] Drizzling this tangy, savory specialty sauce directly onto the crispy cutlet adds an extra layer of umami that elevates the entire dish [[00:43](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DalkyXP1DVRo%26t%3D43)].
