Crossing a Big One Off the Bucket List: Seeing Nippon Professional Baseball Live at the Tokyo Dome! 🏟️⚾️

📍 Tokyo Dome

Watch on YouTube →

Ever since we started planning our adventure in Japan, there was one item sitting right at the top of our family bucket list: attending a Nippon Professional Baseball (NPB) game.

As a family, baseball has always been a huge part of our lives—whether we're playing catch in the yard, following our favorite teams, or spending warm evenings at the ballpark. But we had heard for years that Japanese baseball isn't just a game—it’s an electric, full-body cultural experience.

So, we headed to the iconic Tokyo Dome to watch the famous Yomiuri Giants take the field, and let’s just say: NPB delivered beyond our wild expectations!

Here’s a look into our day at the Tokyo Dome and why Japanese baseball fan culture is something every travel and sports lover needs to experience at least once.



First Impressions: The Energy Before Step One

Walking up to the Tokyo Dome, you can feel the excitement buzzing long before you reach your seats. Bright orange banner displays featuring Giants superstars tagged with "Hero is Here!" lined the walkways outside the stadium.

Inside the concourses, the atmosphere was a seamless blend of traditional ballpark vibes and uniquely Japanese sports culture:



  • Unique Ballpark Eats: From traditional baseball snacks to hearty Japanese curry bowls and gourmet burgers.
  • Special Merch: We loved seeing exclusive stadium gear, including the collaborative Super Mario Bros. × NPB 12-Teams anniversary merch!
  • Pure Nostalgia & Excitement: Spotting iconic character mascots like Mario on the field alongside team favorites got the kids instantly pumped.

NPB Fan Culture: High Energy from the First Pitch to the Last Out

In North America, baseball is often seen as a relaxed, leisurely pastime. In Japan, it is an absolute non-stop celebration!

From the moment the game started, we were blown away by the intensity and coordination in the stands:



1. The Cheering Sections (Ouendan)

The outfield cheering sections are where the heart of Japanese baseball beats loudest. Organized fan groups (ouendan) lead synchronized chants for every single player at bat. They bring trumpets, drums, giant flags, and plastic noisemaker batons. Hearing thousands of fans chant in unison with unbelievable rhythm and zero quiet lulls between innings was unforgettable.



2. Tailored Traditions & Mini Umbrellas

Each team brings its own distinct culture and flair to the stadium. Seeing fans wave colorful mini-umbrellas during stadium rallies and wave team towels in unison during big scoring plays made us realize just how deep and playful fan engagement is here.



3. The Incredible Backpack Beer Vendors (Uriko)

You can't talk about Japanese baseball without mentioning the famous Uriko! Draft beer vendors—mostly energetic young women—walk up and down the steep stadium stairs carrying heavy keg backpacks (draft beer backpacks) and pour fresh, ice-cold draught beer right at your seat with a smile. Their endurance and speed are mind-blowing!



4. Non-Stop Stadium Entertainment

Between cheerleading routines across the field, mascot races, giant screen fan cams, and mid-game festivities, there was never a dull moment. The entire venue felt like one big unified festival.

Final Thoughts: A Must-Do Family Experience in Japan

Seeing a live NPB game at the Tokyo Dome was everything we hoped for and more. Whether you're a die-hard baseball fan like us or just someone who loves experiencing local traditions and high-energy crowds, a Japanese baseball game is an absolute must-add to your Japan itinerary.

We walked out of the stadium with big smiles, sore hands from clapping, and memories that will last a lifetime.
