Is Hama Sushi BETTER Than Sushiro?! 🍣 Conveyor Sushi in Japan!

📍 HAMA-SUSHI Yokosuka Central

Watch on YouTube →

If you’ve spent any time exploring Japan, you know that conveyor belt sushi (kaitenzushi) is an absolute staple of the local food culture. We all have our go-to spots, but today we’re putting one of Japan's biggest sushi chains to the ultimate test: Is Hama Sushi actually BETTER than Sushiro?!

Here is a look inside our latest trip to Hama Sushi and what makes this conveyor sushi experience stand out!

---

1. Seamless Ticketing & Kiosk Check-In 🎟️

From the moment you walk into Hama Sushi, the experience is fast and high-tech. You head straight to the automated ticketing machine at the entrance, grab your seat assignment ticket, and follow the digital guide board directly to your booth.

2. Self-Serve Amenities at Your Table 🍵

Once you get to your booth, everything you need for the perfect meal is right at your fingertips:

Self-Serve Water & Tea: A handy cold water dispenser right at the end of the counter.
 Gari & Condiments: Table containers packed with pickled ginger (gari) and an array of soy sauces and seasonings to customize every bite.

3. High-Speed Direct Delivery & Touchscreen Ordering 🚀

Forget waiting for plates to slowly rotate past your table! At Hama Sushi, you order straight from the digital screen above your booth. Your food arrives on a direct express conveyor lane right to your table level. Touch the screen to confirm your order has arrived, grab your fresh plates, and dive in!

4. More Than Just Sushi: Ramen, Tempura & Creative Toppings 🍜

While classic nigiri is always a hit, Hama Sushi really shines with its variety:

Hot Comfort Food: Piping hot bowls of ramen loaded with fresh greens, corn, and fish cakes.
 Flavor-Packed Nigiri: Creative sushi options featuring chili-oil toppings, fresh daikon radish, and citrusy yuzu-topped salmon.
Crispy Sides: Crispy, golden tempura and kakiage* fritters served fresh and hot.

---

The Verdict: Hama Sushi vs. Sushiro

Both chains have a special place in our hearts, but Hama Sushi’s combination of direct express delivery lanes, extensive side menu options, and variety of special sauces definitely gives Sushiro a run for its money!
