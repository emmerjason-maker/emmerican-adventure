Layover Eats at Seoul Incheon (ICN): Lounge Buffets and Unique Dunkin’ Finds

📍 Incheon International Airport

Connecting through Seoul Incheon International Airport (ICN) Terminal 2 gives you plenty of solid food options before boarding a long-haul flight. Whether you are relaxing in an airport lounge or browsing the concourse for a quick snack, Incheon turns a routine layover into a surprisingly fun food stop.

What’s on the Menu in the Lounges

If you have lounge access through Priority Pass, Matina Lounge—located upstairs on the fourth floor above the luxury shops in Terminal 2—operates much more like a full buffet restaurant than a typical grab-and-go airline club.

The hot food spread blends Korean comfort favorites with familiar Asian and Western staples:

  • Savory Hot Plates: You can load up on vegetable fried rice, sweet-and-spicy Korean fried chicken, tempura-fried bites, and chewy tteokbokki (spicy rice cakes).

  • Hearty Comfort Food: Alongside the Korean dishes, the buffet regularly features Japanese-style vegetable curry over steamed rice with bright red fukujinzuke pickles, crispy breaded croquettes drizzled with savory sauce, and stir-fried noodles and vegetables.

  • Sweet Finishes: For dessert, glass display cases hold bowls of colorful macarons—in shades of bright blue, green, and yellow—alongside cakes, pastries, and fresh fruit.

Dunkin’ Korea: Way Beyond Standard Donuts

If you take a walk through the terminal concourse, stopping by Dunkin’ in South Korea is worth it just to see how different the menu is from locations back in the U.S. In addition to classic glazed rings and colorful gourmet donuts, the Korean menu leans heavily into creative savory sandwiches and local collaborations:

  • Hakhwa Walnut Munchkins (Hakhwa Hodo Munchkin): Dunkin’ teamed up with the historic Korean walnut pastry brand Hakhwa Hodugwaja (established in 1934) to create powdered donut holes stuffed with sweet red bean paste and walnuts—putting a modern twist on Korea’s classic highway rest-stop treat.

  • Corn Cheese Croque Monsieur: Created in collaboration with chef Austin Kang, this toasted sandwich pairs sweet corn with gooey, melted mozzarella cheese—bringing one of Korea’s favorite bar snacks to the airport breakfast lineup.

  • Savory Sandwich Lineup: Alongside standard bacon-and-egg muffins, the menu board features items like a Jambong Ham & Cheese Ciabatta, Cranberry Chicken Croissant Sandwich, Egg Burrito, and even a Classic Hot Dog.

  • Unique Drinks: Alongside standard espresso drinks and iced lattes, you can order a "Giant Bucket Americano" or refreshing fruit-forward options like Yuja (citron) or Grapefruit Chamomile tea and Plain Yogurt Coolattas.

Between a full plate of hot food in the lounge and a box of red-bean walnut Munchkins for the gate, a layover at Incheon is one of the easiest places in Asia to eat well before takeoff.
